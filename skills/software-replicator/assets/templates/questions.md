# Open questions for the owner

**Never ask how the new system should look or behave. The answer is always: exactly like the source.**
If you do not know what the source does, go observe it (open the screen, hover the element, trigger the
state, read the CSS), do not put it here. This list is ONLY for operational things the source cannot
answer: which account/login to use, where the app repo is, data-migration scope and timing, cutover
timing, whether it will be sold (legal), and screens your account genuinely cannot reach (ask the owner
to SHOW you what the source does there, with a screenshot or recording, not what they would prefer).

Batched, plain English, recommendation first. Ask the whole batch at a stop point, never one at a time
mid-build. Move answered items to decisions.md.
